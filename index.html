About Us

Cold Box Core Shooter Manufacturer in India

Galaxy Machines is a leading manufacturer of foundry equipment in India. We specialise in manufacturing a wide range of special purpose machines and supporting equipments. We are well known for our best quality products with robust designs, excellent workmanship, high efficiency and maximum productivity. With our team of highly qualified experts, we manufacture custom made machines tailored to all the special needs of our customers. Galaxy Machines specializes in manufacturing cold box and shell core shooters. We look after customer needs right from design and manufacturing up to transport, installation and after sales support for complete customer satisfaction.
